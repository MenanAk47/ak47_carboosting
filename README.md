**The ultimate car boosting and vehicular heist experience for FiveM servers.** Ak47 Car Boosting completely revamps vehicle theft on your server with an ultra-sleek Cyberpunk Car Boosting OS tablet, tiered contracts, dynamic squad co-op heists, and intense police chases. ## 🔥 Key Features

Designed to improve roleplay immersion and economy balancing:

### 📱 Next-Gen Cyberpunk Tablet & OS

* **Custom Driver Profile:** Operatives register with custom aliases and image URLs, tracking career XP, tier rankings, and completed heist records.
* **Contract Dispatch Stream:** Intercept incoming vehicle heist contracts with toggleable scanner queues, dynamic expiration timers, and payout previews.

### 👥 Squad Heists & Tiered Progression

* **Tiered Progression (D to S Class):** Unlock higher-value sports and supercars as you gain XP, with strict tier requirements and difficulty scaling.
* **Co-op Squad Operations:** Form crews with friends, send join requests, and fulfill tier squad capacity requirements (solo up to 4-man crews) with split cash and crypto payouts.

### 🚨 High-Stakes Infiltration & Live GPS Hacking

* **Garage Door & Vehicle Security:** Break into locked commercial garages and electronic car security systems using specialized hacking tablets and lockpicks.
* **Real-Time Moving GPS Tracker:** Passengers must complete multi-stage scrambler minigames while on the run to kill the vehicle's tracker before police zero in.
* **Armed NPC Defenders:** High-tier contracts spawn hostile security guards equipped with body armor, shotguns, and assault rifles guarding the target vehicle.

### 🏎️ VIN Scratching & Black Market Hardware

* **Permanent Vehicle Ownership (VIN Scratch):** Forfeit the cash bounty to scrub the VIN number, generate fresh plates, and register the stolen vehicle straight into your garage.
* **Black Market Hardware Shop:** Purchase hacking tablets, lockpicks, and performance parts with cash or earned special crypto coins, then collect them at physical dealer drop-offs.
* **Usable Performance Upgrades:** Install tuning parts (engines, turbos, brakes, suspensions, armor, window tints, 13 xenon light colors) anywhere with immersive installation animations.

### 🛠️ Technical Excellence

* **Multi-Framework & Target Support:** Seamlessly works with ESX, QB, QBX, and various target systems (ox_target, qb-target, qtarget).
* **Highly Secure:** Built-in server-side exploit checks, including distance validation and trigger authentication, to prevent cheating.
* **Comprehensive Server Integrations:** Automated database setup, configurable leaderboard season resets, custom dispatch integration (cd_dispatch, cd_dispatch3d), synced animations, and Discord webhooks.

## ⚙️ How To Install:

* Download ak47_carboosting from your cfx portal.
* Add the script in your resources folder.
* Add items based on your framework instruction.
* Add item images into inventory.
* Start the script in your server.cfg below framework files.
* Make sure you have all dependencies.
* Restart the server.
